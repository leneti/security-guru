# ADR 0002: Serve homes and offices equally; remove dead ends

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-28

## Context

Security Guru serves homeowners and offices. The current external shop no longer exists, and the Projects page is a placeholder.

## Decision

Give home and office visitors equal prominence. Do not include the external shop or a Projects page in the new navigation or page plan. Keep the five service categories: intrusion alarms, access control, fire alarms, video surveillance, and integrated security solutions. Keep a clear route to contact the company.

Vilnius and Vilnius County are the confirmed service area.

## Consequences

- Proposed sitemap: Home; Services overview; five service detail pages; About; Contact.
- Home and office needs should appear as parallel examples or entry points within that structure; separate audience microsites are not required at the outset.
- Retain or redirect useful existing service URLs when the final route map is approved. Remove obsolete shop and Projects links from navigation and generated sitemaps.
