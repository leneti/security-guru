# ADR 0004: Edit Lithuanian copy; retain starting prices

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-28

## Context

Existing Lithuanian prose can be revised. Service prices are actual starting prices, with detailed quotes expected after an enquiry. Some other copy contains claims and operational details that may have changed.

## Decision

Treat existing prose as editable source material. Keep starting price presentation for the services and make it clear that a final price depends on the project. Confirm the amounts before publication. Vilnius and Vilnius County coverage is confirmed; do not present other unverified claims, hours, or contact details as freshly confirmed facts.

## Consequences

- Current starting prices to verify: intrusion alarm €200; access control €100; fire alarm €200; video surveillance €200; integrated solutions €200. The integrated solution price appears in the service card but not its detail page.
- Rewrite generic or awkward copy for clarity, while preserving business meaning and obtaining review for factual changes.
- Check the cited 300% burglary-risk statistic and the fire-system regulatory statement before using either in final copy.
- Confirm the current phone numbers, email, and 9:00–21:00 daily hours.
